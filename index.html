MojiMask

Hide words inside an emoji

Write a message, lock it with a password, and hide it inside any emoji you choose. Send that emoji to a friend. Only the password opens what’s inside.

Use your phone’s emoji keyboard. On Windows press Win + . and on Mac press Ctrl + Cmd + Space.
Password strength

The hidden part, drawn as glyphs:

Encryption uses AES-256-GCM with a key made from your password by PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 600,000 rounds). Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. Lost passwords can’t be recovered, and the expiry is checked by the app, so treat it as a courtesy lock, not a guarantee. Some chat apps remove invisible characters. If the receiver sees “No hidden message found”, send the emoji again through another app.
